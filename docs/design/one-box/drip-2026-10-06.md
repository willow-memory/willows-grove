# The fat, dripped (2026-10-06)

*Desk session, 2026-10-06, at the operator's word: "go crazy with it. Let the
fat drip." This is the third grid. Rows R–Z come after the first grid's A–M
and the second's N–Q, and Z is the last letter, so the alphabet is full and
this is where this set of grids stops. Agent-reported; not ratified.*

## What dripped

The measure named the fat boxes: the ones holding the most text, which are
often several rules sharing one box. Eight boxes in the first grid hold at
least 2× an even share (G5, K10, L6, J2, J9, G4, J7, B11). The second grid
has none that fat, so its fattest box, P1, was added at 1.5×. Nine parents
fill the nine letters R–Z, largest first.

`cross_table.py drip` cut each parent's passage into clauses: a clause runs
to its sentence's full stop, or to a table-cell boundary. List numbers and
abbreviations don't end a clause. Each clause got its own box, and its own
pattern that finds it again from the top of the source file, **at its own
place**. A clause that can't be pinned there is silent and says so. That's
why S1 ("D10") and S10 ("Phase 7") are empty: the same words appear earlier
in the file, in another row. Nothing is composed. The row titles are the
parents' labels, and each cell's label is the first five words of its
clause.

The width is the longest parent: K10 cut into 10 clauses. Shorter parents
leave the rest of their row silent ("no more clauses"). The measure below
shows that honestly. A grid of clauses is thin by design.

Each row is linked to its parent in [the crosswalk](crosswalk-2026-10-06.md).

## Where the dripping stops

Measured, this grid is fat again: R2, R3, R5, R6, S6, S7, U2, V1, W1, W2 and
Y2 each hold at least 3× an even share. But each of those is already a single
clause, so cutting it again gives back the same box. The fat that's left is
real weight, one long sentence each, and not several rules sharing a box.
That, and running out of letters at Z, is where it stops. The Gini of 0.74 is
mostly the silent tail: 52 empty boxes in short rows count as holding
nothing.
