## The notes (the source)

### IZ · 113 and the trigger

- **The opening ask.** "Tell me, just from training and what's in the box, about the number 113." The 30th prime, an emirp, 355/113 for π, nihonium; in the box only incidental hits.
- **What the box held.** `203.0.113.x` (TEST-NET-3) in three loopback tests; `ideas.md` #113, FRANK's "the chain is intact. but are *you*?"; `integrations.py:113`, a vault check that logs at debug and returns `None`; a drawio arrow at `y="113"`.
- **The quiet vault check.** `credential_source()` reports a broken vault as no credential, where `_read_vault` warns: unreachable collapsed into empty. Held for the operator, not fixed.
- **The trigger set.** "So the next time a natural 113 appears, not something that's in documents not something that we would naturally produce in our conversation or by using github, the next time it appears we will open the pr". Then "keep it at 113" after a natural 13 and a 130.
- **The guesses frozen.** Seven guesses with percentages, amended once (Was/Now), then "we're not going to go through that cycle." Checked later beside the frozen file: 5 of 8 held or strengthened.

### JA · Tables, percentages, and the rules

- **The table rule.** "write every output in table and or full readme Style markdown." Written into CLAUDE.md as Rule 7.
- **Percentages only.** "No high medium low no other qualifiers except percentages and or extended percentages off those percentages i.e. 88.03%"
- **Commit, push, and the hook.** "We are also not going to go to push after every prompt. That's stupid." then "It would be commit, but not push." The environment's stop hook asked about 58 times; the operator's word held: "Mine is the verify 'done' hook. Mine is stronger."
- **Rules linked to the constitution.** "Even better, just a short description, and a link to what and where". CLAUDE.md became one line per rule with its Draft 0.9 clause, framed for any agent, from 107 lines to 73.
- **The Watch reserved.** "I think it might be time to cut heim from the desk, all together. I have a different watch in mind." Cut from CLAUDE.md only; the rest of the repo untouched.

### JB · Verifying a Gemini output

- **The claim checked.** A pasted Gemini answer put the odds of a conversation at 1 in 1.25 trillion. The arithmetic held (8 × 10⁻¹³); every factor was invented.
- **The acoustics.** A 10-foot room's first axial mode is c/2d = 56.5 Hz, not 113 Hz; 113 Hz is +46.58 cents above A2 (110 Hz), not a slightly sharp orchestral A (440 Hz).
- **The key.** The Axis of Awesome "Four Chords" is in E major (E–B–C♯m–A), not rooted on A.
- **The method.** The choices were not independent, and the target was picked after the fact: a measure of how specific the description is, not how rare the conversation was.
- **Looked up.** "Look up the questions on the internet." Speed of sound about 1,125 ft/s at 20 °C; the film's tones G–A–F–F–C. Sources kept in the session record.

### JC · The quorum ruling

- **The ruling.** "So a quorum must hold at least three parties. One being neutral, agreed on by the other two parties."
- **Mediation, not a vote.** "It doesn't matter. Both parties are right in their own head, and that is what mediation is about. If the parties can't agree, both get recorded and marked as unresolved. And that they need to escalate to The Next Step Up."
- **The record first.** "I would say, The record is read first is the most important out of all those." That is IV.8, the recorded before the new, in the order the human sets.
- **Where it lands.** The agent's reading: Article VII's Court of Last Resort, a fresh, memoryless interpreter, with the record paying its cost of re-reasoning every case. Whether it answers Open Decision #1 is the operator's.
- **Answers from other humans.** "Lots of humans have given decisions on things." Court practice for the open questions: the next step up appoints a neutral, one impasse escalates, prior rulings are read first. Unratified.
