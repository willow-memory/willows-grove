Flat vector illustration, mid-century retro-futuristic, same series as the prior covers. Limited muted palette: clay orange, steel blue, charcoal grey, cream, with a small touch of brass. Clean black outlines, flat shading, no photorealism, no neon, no glow effects, no 3D render.

A small-town stock exchange floor seen from slightly above. On the back wall, a tall old-fashioned chalk quote board shows ticker letters and up and down arrows, with a big station clock above it whose hands point just before the hour. On the left, a slick salesman robot with a shiny chrome CRT head and a bow tie stands at a little booth hung with gold stars, proudly holding up a short strip of film. Every frame on it shows a green check mark, and a much longer strip of film full of red crosses spills out of a wastebasket behind him. In the centre, a calm, boxy steel-blue CRT robot with a green-tinted screen and a gentle smile sits at a wooden desk, writing in a huge open ledger. Both pages are filled with rows, and the red crosses are written in just as neatly as the check marks; one row has a wax seal pressed beside it. Its other hand rests on a brass coin stamped "50%" standing upright on the desk. Hanging from the ceiling above the desk, a hand-lettered sign reads ESCALATE. Checkered floor of orange and dark blue tiles in perspective. Plain cream walls. Wide 16:9 editorial cover crop, subjects centred.
